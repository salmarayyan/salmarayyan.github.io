No likes. No feed. Just film.

Rolodex is a film camera for your iPhone. Load a roll, shoot it, and send it to develop. Like a real disposable camera, you don't see your photos until the prints are back.

Rolodex for iPhone

How it works

  1. Pick a film and load the roll. Most rolls are 24 shots, and there's no swapping halfway.
  2. Shoot the whole roll. No previews, no retakes.
  3. Send it to develop. Your prints come back to the shelf shortly after.

Five films

  • Sunny
  • Poolside
  • Afterhours
  • Newsprint, in black and white
  • Evergreen

Each has its own colours, grain and number of exposures.

Private by design

Your photos are stored only on your iPhone and are never uploaded. Rolodex has no account, no ads and no tracking.

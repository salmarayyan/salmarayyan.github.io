privacy

rolodex · updated 1 november 2026

the short version

your photos stay on your phone. rolodex has no account, no tracking, no ads and no analytics. we don't collect anything.

your photos

photos are taken, stored and developed entirely on your device. they are never uploaded, collected, or shared with us or anyone else, unless you share them yourself.

camera

the camera is used only to take photos for your roll.

photo library

rolodex asks for add-only access, and only when you save. it can put prints into your library; it can't see or read anything already in there.

location

rolodex doesn't use your location.

notifications

if you allow them, rolodex sends one local notification when a roll is back from the lab. it's scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent to a server.

data we collect

none. no names, emails, device ids, usage data or crash reports leave the app. if that ever changes, this page changes first.

deleting your data

throw a roll away on the shelf, or delete the app, and its photos are gone from rolodex. prints you saved to photos stay in your library.

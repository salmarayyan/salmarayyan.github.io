Privacy Policy

Rolodex · Effective 7 November 2026

1. Introduction

Rolodex is a film-camera app for iPhone, made by Salma Rayyan. This policy explains what information Rolodex does and doesn't collect, how your photos are handled, and how to get in touch. It applies to the Rolodex app and to this website, salmarayyan.com.

2. Information We Collect

None. Rolodex has no account and no sign-in, and the developer collects no personal information, usage data, or device identifiers of any kind.

3. Device Permissions

Rolodex asks for a small number of permissions, each for a single purpose. Camera: used only to take photos inside the app. Photo library: Rolodex requests add-only access, and only when you choose to save. This lets it add prints to your library but does not let it see or read the photos already there. Notifications: optional. If you allow them, Rolodex schedules one local notification on your device when a roll has finished developing. Nothing is sent to a server. Rolodex does not use your location, microphone, or contacts.

4. How Your Photos Are Stored

Every photo you take is stored and processed entirely on your device. Your photos are never uploaded, collected, or shared with the developer or anyone else, unless you choose to share them yourself.

5. Third Parties

Rolodex uses no third-party SDKs or services and shares no data with third parties. If you have turned on "Share With App Developers" in iOS Settings, Apple may provide the developer with anonymous crash reports. This is controlled by Apple, not by Rolodex, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

6. Tracking and Advertising

Rolodex contains no ads, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Because Rolodex collects no data, there is nothing for the developer to retain or delete. Your photos stay on your device for as long as you keep them. Throwing a roll away on the shelf, or deleting the app, removes its photos from Rolodex. Prints you have saved to Photos remain in your library until you delete them there.

8. Withdrawing Permissions

You can change or withdraw any permission at any time in iOS Settings > Rolodex. Turning off the camera will prevent you from taking photos, and turning off photo library access will prevent saving, but the app will otherwise continue to work.

9. Children's Privacy

Rolodex does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

11. Contact Us

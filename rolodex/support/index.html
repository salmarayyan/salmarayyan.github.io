how does it work?

load a film, shoot the roll, and send it to the darkroom. you can't see your photos until the roll is developed, just like a disposable camera.

where are my photos?

developed rolls live on the shelf (the stack of bangles in the top corner). open a roll to flip through its prints.

can i see a photo before the roll is done?

no peeking, that's the point. if you can't wait, hold to rewind early and develop the roll with the shots you've taken.

how do i save prints to my photos?

open a roll on the shelf and tap save, or turn on auto-save rolls in settings so every roll goes to photos once it's developed.

the camera or saving isn't working

check rolodex is allowed to use the camera and add photos: settings app › rolodex. then close and reopen the app.

how do i see the tutorial again?

settings › replay tutorial.

Contact

If you need help with Rolodex, email salmarayyann@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, your iOS version and a short description of the problem. I aim to reply within 2–3 business days. You can also tap Contact / Feedback in the app's Settings.

How does Rolodex work?

Load a film, shoot the whole roll, then send it to develop. Like a real disposable camera, you can't see your photos until the roll is developed.

Which films are there?

There are five film looks: Sunny, Holocene, Poolside, Afterhours and Newsprint (black and white). Each has its own colours and grain, and every roll is 24 shots. You choose one when you load a roll and can't swap halfway.

Can I see my photos before the roll is finished?

No peeking, that's the point. Finish the roll, send it to develop, and your prints are back shortly after.

Where are my photos?

Developed rolls live on the shelf. Tap the shelf button in the top-left corner, then open a roll to flip through its prints.

How do I save photos to my library?

Open a roll and tap Save, or turn on Auto-save Rolls in the app's Settings so every roll is saved to Photos once it's developed.

The camera or saving isn't working

Go to iPhone Settings > Rolodex and make sure Camera and Photos access are allowed, then close and reopen the app.

How do I see the tutorial again?

In the app, open Settings > Replay Tutorial.

How do I delete photos?

On the shelf, throw away a roll to delete its photos from Rolodex. Prints you've already saved to Photos stay in your library.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your photos are stored only on your iPhone and are never uploaded. Rolodex has no account, no ads and no tracking. Read the full Privacy Policy.

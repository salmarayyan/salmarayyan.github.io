How does Rolodex work?

Load a film, shoot the roll, and develop it. Your photos stay hidden until the roll is developed, just like a disposable camera.

Where are my photos?

Developed rolls live on the shelf. Tap the button in the top-left corner to open it.

Can I see photos before the roll is finished?

No. If you can't wait, hold to rewind early and the roll will be developed sooner with the shots you have taken.

How do I save photos to my library?

Open a roll and tap Save. To save every roll automatically once it is developed, turn on Auto-save Rolls in the app's Settings.

The camera or saving isn't working

Open iOS Settings > Rolodex and make sure Camera and Photos are allowed. Then close and reopen the app.

How do I replay the tutorial?

In the app, go to Settings > Replay Tutorial.

How do I delete my photos?

Throw a roll away on the shelf, or delete the app, and its photos are removed from Rolodex. Prints you have saved to Photos stay in your library until you delete them there.
